Additional context & model assumptions

EEG ending at the selected first-word cutoff is summarized at multiple time scales. A compact model maps it into a semantic embedding. Noisy channels remain masked.

Across-app capture

Starts only when you press Start. A menu-bar indicator provides Stop & save. Captures new keyboard input in accessible text fields; skips password/protected fields, terminals, clipboard/paste, and unsupported composition. After switching fields or using a shortcut, the first partial word is skipped until a space. Words are reconstructed from keys, so another app’s autocorrection is not observed. Everything stays local.

All text and EEG stay on this computer.

Technical evaluation

Free writing only. A frozen local MiniLM encoder supplies 384-dimensional phrase targets. A regularized EEG model predicts that space; results retrieve earlier phrases rather than generate new text. Candidate cosine similarities are not probabilities. Unvalidated matches are marked explicitly.

Later phrases are evaluated after a 60-second gap against mean-embedding, previous-text and channel-quality baselines. Exact phrase repeats are excluded from validation. Motor and eye activity may still contribute; passing these checks does not establish thought decoding.

Training reads individual EEG files from disk, uses at most 512 phrases from the most recent 3,000 words, and runs in a separate CPU process with one compute thread, batches of eight text embeddings, a 1.5 GiB worker memory watchdog and a three-minute time limit. New continuous EEG uses lossless gzip. Recording stops below 10 GiB free; no recordings are automatically deleted.

Getting a clean signal

  1. Rest forehead sensors against clean, dry skin.
  2. Move hair away from both ear contacts; adjust fit gently.
  3. Relax your jaw. Sit still, look at fixation, and avoid blinking during trials.
  4. Blink during breaks. Inspect all four traces before starting.

Contact is estimated from EEG, not measured impedance. Muse does not provide an impedance value through this bridge.

Import and standardize images

Images are converted to grayscale, fitted without distortion, padded to 512 × 512, and normalized to target digital mean 0.5 and RMS contrast 0.18. Achieved values and clipping are saved. This alone does not establish publication-level matching of spatial frequency, content or familiarity.
